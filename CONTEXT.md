# Forever Tracker

A shared record of one group of friends' journey through WoW Forever. It celebrates what the group accomplishes together and does not rank its members.

## Language

### People

**Group**:
The friends who play WoW Forever together and whom the tracker is about. The Group is the main character of the product.
_Avoid_: Roster, party, guild, team

**Player**:
A real person in the Group.
_Avoid_: User, member

**Character**:
A Player's in-game hero, followed through the armory.
_Avoid_: Toon, profile

**Main**:
The one Character a Player brings on the Journey. A Player who rerolls switches Main and keeps their history; alts are not followed.
_Avoid_: Active character

### The journey

**Journey**:
Everything the Group goes through together, from level 1 onwards. It has no finish line.
_Avoid_: Race, progress, standings

**Chapter**:
One stage of the Journey, such as levelling to 60, dungeons, raiding or PvP. Reaching 60 closes one Chapter, not the Journey.
_Avoid_: Phase, season

**Milestone**:
A noteworthy moment on the Journey, noticed by comparing what the armory shows now with what it showed before.
_Avoid_: Event, achievement (that word belongs to the game's own achievements)

**Personal Milestone**:
A Milestone reached by one Main, such as reaching level 40, a reputation tier, or a first Epic item. It is told as part of the Group's story, never compared.

**Group Milestone**:
A Milestone that belongs to the whole Group, such as levels earned together passing 200 or every Main being mounted.

**Baseline**:
What the tracker sees the first time it looks at a Main, or at something new about it. A Baseline is never a Milestone; only changes after it are.

**Levels earned together**:
The sum of every Main's levels gained since level 1. It only ever grows.
_Avoid_: Group average, total level

**Journey log**:
The Group's Milestones in time order, told as one shared story.
_Avoid_: Feed, activity, history

### Playing together

**Dungeon suggestion**:
A dungeon whose recommended level range currently includes at least two Mains of the same faction, shown with who fits.
_Avoid_: Level band, matchmaking

### The game

**Quality**:
The game's grade of an item or mount: Poor, Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic or Legendary, each shown in its own colour (grey, white, green, blue, purple, orange). A Milestone about gear or mounts names the items or mounts that earned it, in their Quality colour.
_Avoid_: Tier, rarity
